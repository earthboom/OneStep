# Clarify weapon placement and release of a prolonged greeting pose

## Expected and observed

002 keeps the sword drawn through death confirmation, then tears cloth and binds an arm before later sheathing. 003 releases a salute only after extended dialogue. These are clarity concerns, not demonstrated physical impossibilities.

## Cause and resolution

The observable omissions are the interim prop state and delayed pose update. 002 now puts the sword at the feet after death confirmation and picks it up after a companion assumes the courtyard. 003 lowers the hands after self-introduction and removes the later duplicated release.

## Recheck and prevention

Both editorial changes are resolved. The review check remains proposed for future scenes; no universal pose duration or weapon-handling rule is inferred.
