# A Part I estimate omitted the intended serial scale and ensemble progression

## Expected and observed

The author clarified that Part I should run at least 150 installments, with a minimum length per installment. The intended part lets an ensemble train, fail and earn local successes before its largest crisis. The author rejected the short estimate and clarified that the final rescue should itself span several dozen installments. The original prompt did not supply a 150-installment requirement in advance of the estimate.

## Cause and resolution

The estimate used a narrower view of the arc than the author intended. The revised working outline uses installments 1–150 as a planning scale, allows longer individual installments, and develops several local events before convergence. It allocates 110–144 to the rescue and 145–150 to aftermath. The journey design gives the segments different changes in information, options or responsibilities.

## Recheck and prevention

The current chapter-range table includes smaller events and closes with consequences, rather than ending at passage through the last obstacle. The result meets the requested planning scale. The repeated-motion check is proposed for later chapter design and prose review. The actual pacing, exact installments and reader response have not been verified.

## Evidence

- Novel_0, novel-2. Author feedback on the Part I estimate, growth of the younger ensemble and scale of the final siege, in the project discussion.
- 2026-11-20 Part I 150-installment outline, opening chapter-range table, and the final siege outline, “35-installment journey and changes in the siege.” Original plans remain in the working project.
