# Summoned-person label disagreed with the arrival sequence

## Expected and observed

The narrator is already at the gate. A novice runs inside; the senior brother then appears. The phrase 나를 불러온 제자 assigns the summons to the narrator instead.

## Cause and resolution

The demonstrated cause is a textual mismatch between the role label and the shown event. No claim is made about an internal model cause. It now reads 사형을 불러온 제자. An unshown separate summons is unsupported.

## Recheck and prevention

The gate-to-corridor reunion was reread against the original opening: the same novice can now fetch the senior brother and subsequently carry the return message to the elders. Correction is resolved; broader future prevention remains proposed, not automatically enforced.

Prevention state is proposed. The current correction or documented risk does not establish automatic future enforcement. The check is conditional on the same actor, task, and information context.
