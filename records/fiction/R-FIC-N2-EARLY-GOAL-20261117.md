# An early-scene review assigned a later objective before it formed

## Expected and observed

The opening reunion and decision to stay needed to reflect the protagonist's circumstances at that time. The prior review recommended a hint of the later institutional goal at the end of the reunion. The author questioned why that goal would already be visible. The review documents the withdrawn recommendation.

## Cause and resolution

The review conflated the author's long-term design with an explicit intention already held by the character. The old overall outline also described the goal as running from the first event. The corrected sequence lets return, daily company and later practical experience accrue before the character identifies a new objective. An early act of respecting an existing duty-holder can retain its ordinary motive.

## Recheck and prevention

The corrected opening review and subsequent outline revision agree that the character's initial decision is to stay. Later experiences supply the reason for the larger goal. The prevention check dates what the character knows and wants, and checks related summaries for the same boundary. It is proposed for later drafts. Resolution covers the advice and outline conflict.
