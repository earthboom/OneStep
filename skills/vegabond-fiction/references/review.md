# Manuscript review and correction

Read this from [vegabond-fiction](../SKILL.md) before judging or revising a manuscript. The [agent instructions](../../../agents/vegabond-agent.md) own common completion and acceptance; [fiction-design](../../vegabond-fiction-design/SKILL.md) owns narrative choices, and this reference owns their assessment in actual text.

## Review and verify

Review story-level problems before polishing expression; a short text can combine these in one pass. Apply the following checks to the requested scope and form:

| Area | Check |
| --- | --- |
| Causality and motivation | What desires, information, pressures, or triggers support a choice and its consequences? Do not supply missing motivation yourself or confuse an irrational character choice with a narrative logic error. |
| Verisimilitude | Do actions, reactions, emotions, and details support belief within this genre, world, and situation? Judge against established conditions rather than personal taste or everyday realism alone. |
| Continuity | Do established facts, character knowledge, chronology, location, world rules, and setups/payoffs agree under the same conditions? Check viewpoint and narrator reliability before treating conflicting statements as conflicting facts. |
| Reader understanding | Using only information revealed by this point, can the intended reader follow the necessary connections? Distinguish a deliberately unanswered question from a missing connection that prevents understanding the scene; later revelations must not be treated as already known. |
| Style | Compare viewpoint, tense, tone, narrator/character voices, and pacing with the work's baseline. Check interchangeable character speech, uniform paragraph patterns, and habitual explanatory scene endings against scene purpose. Preserve deliberate ambiguity, dialect, repetition, and unconventional expression. |
| Sentences and words | Check explanations that repeat what action or dialogue already conveys, generic reactions interchangeable across characters, and imagery or sensory detail without a scene function. Select detail through what this viewpoint character would notice now; preserve meaning and useful rhythm. |
| Orthography and form | Spelling, spacing, punctuation, naming conventions, requested length, and formatting follow the work's language and agreed conventions. Check automated suggestions against context and intentional usage. |

Read the complete final text within scope, not just edited lines, and inspect affected surrounding passages. Identify conclusions that require unavailable chapters. Explain editorial judgments with cited passages and concrete reader effects; distinguish confirmed errors, improvement suggestions, and unresolved hypotheses. Mechanical checks assist this reading but cannot establish literary quality.

Compare the final scene's factual claims and state changes with the retrieved context and governing sources, including character knowledge, relationship direction, and disclosure timing. Flag newly introduced setting facts separately so that a plausible draft does not silently redefine established canon.

After story-level repairs, use these style checks in the same review cycle and reread the final text for flow against the baseline. Treat "AI-like" as a request to diagnose concrete prose effects, not evidence of authorship or a sufficient finding by itself. Judge repetitions by their function and context rather than a universal word blacklist. Do not manufacture naturalness through typos, arbitrary fragments, slang, mechanical sentence-length variation, or forced novelty. Direct emotion and concise exposition remain valid when they serve the scene; do not convert every feeling into an action.

## Judge uncertain findings

For a disputed plausibility or interpretation finding, use a short reasoning chain: **passage -> applicable criterion -> textual evidence -> plausible alternative reading -> judgment and smallest useful change**. Consider a text-supported alternative before declaring an error. Do not invent hidden backstory or canon to excuse a gap, and do not add explanations that disclose a planned reveal prematurely.

- **Confirmed error:** a demonstrated contradiction or violated requirement after relevant context and intentional exceptions are checked. Correct it within the authorized editing scope and recheck.
- **Unresolved hypothesis:** missing context or competing supported readings prevent a firm diagnosis. Inspect the relevant evidence; otherwise state the uncertainty and avoid rewriting as though the diagnosis were established.
- **Improvement suggestion or preference:** explain the intended reader effect and the tradeoff. Apply it when supported by the requested editing direction and preservation conditions; otherwise present it as an option.

Keep confidence separate from impact: a consequential suspicion is not automatically a confirmed error. Explain uncertainty when material without invented numerical scores. Grammar and orthography checks also respect dialogue, dialect, and deliberate stylistic exceptions.

Do not claim universal or majority reader agreement without actual audience evidence. Simulated reader perspectives may expose alternative interpretations, but agreement among AI passes is not a reader survey. If audience response is essential to an acceptance condition, use available relevant reader feedback or report that evidence gap; do not add a routine reader-approval checkpoint to every task.

## Record findings

Keep a compact problem list in the task's existing report or record system. Each finding names the passage, review area, evidence or editorial rationale, finding type, proposed correction, and resolution/recheck result. For disputed findings, retain the material uncertainty, alternative reading, and impact with the rationale above. Group minor instances of the same problem where useful; do not create a permanent lesson for each typo.

Provide these findings to vegabond-knowledge, which owns storage, targeted retrieval, deduplication, and retirement within the task's write scope. Supply story-specific applicability and recurrence-prevention checks for reusable lessons, including the relevant scene and reader-information context. Keep tentative diagnoses in the current task history; an author or story preference must remain scoped to that author or story rather than becoming a universal rule or a defect merely because the author selected another approach.

For a reusable style correction, include before/after passage locations, the diagnosed effect, why the change fits the baseline, preservation/recheck evidence, and any actual author feedback. Keep AI review and author acceptance distinct. Return work-specific style conventions and confirmed reusable corrections through the existing knowledge/record lifecycle, without a separate style store or schema. On later writing requests, retrieve the applicable baseline and correction checks through the context request above; use them conditionally rather than copying wording. If the author prefers the original or changes direction, retain that decision and update the affected convention and brief instead of reapplying the rejected correction.

## Correct and repeat

For drafting or authorized revision, apply the finding classification above before rewriting. Repair the earliest established causal problem at the appropriate level while preserving the agreed intent and constraints. Prefer the smallest sufficient cue, action, dialogue, or ordering change; more exposition is not automatically a better repair. Reread affected scenes and transitions for new contradictions or disrupted rhythm, checking whether clarity improved without damaging tension, reveals, voice, or characterization. When a fact or event changes, trace consequences through relevant character knowledge, relationships, chronology, and later references. Within authorized scope, reconcile established changes with existing setting/continuity sources and pass evidence to vegabond-knowledge for affected entry and catalog maintenance. Keep provisional inventions marked as such; do not silently promote them or overwrite source conflicts. Refresh affected context before the next dependent scene rather than carrying forward a stale brief.

Repeat affected checks after each repair, revisiting expression and orthography after structural changes. Before handoff, read the complete final text within scope again. Expand beyond that scope only to inspect actual dependencies; do not restart the whole manuscript on every local edit. If a problem persists, reassess the diagnosis and correction against the intended effect instead of accumulating rewrites without progress.

Finish when the requested writing goal and constraints are met and confirmed in-scope errors are resolved. Account for material reader-understanding risks with evidence, correction, or an explicit unresolved condition; a preference difference alone does not prevent completion. Report remaining editorial choices and unavailable evidence rather than endlessly polishing or seeking universal agreement. If missing intent or material prevents judging a required condition, report that condition under the agent's completion contract instead of claiming verification complete.

For critique-only or research work, verify and correct the findings rather than rewriting the manuscript. Hand back the requested text or analysis, important editorial choices, problem resolutions and recheck evidence, reusable prevention references, and open questions. The agent instructions govern vagabond's independent review and explicit acceptance.
