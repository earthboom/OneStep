# Planning review

Assess a proposal or execution plan against the intended outcome, constraints and available resources. Use relevant sections of the existing [planning review](../../vegabond-planning/references/review.md); assessing a plan does not authorize executing it.

- Trace problem, intervention, expected change and outcome. Separate evidence of need from preferences or requests for a solution.
- Reconcile scope, effort, elapsed time, capacity, budget, owners and dependencies. Check units, arithmetic and cost horizon. An assigned name is not evidence of a confirmed commitment.
- Test load-bearing assumptions with available evidence or a conditional calculation. Identify a useful validation, observable decision criterion and response if the assumption fails. Leave unknown inputs unknown.
- Compare real alternatives when material, including a minimal change where viable. Explain tradeoffs and the assumption that would reverse the recommendation.
- Separate delivery milestones from outcomes. For important metrics, check baseline, numerator/denominator, population, measurement period and method. A launch or proxy metric does not establish the intended benefit or causation.
- Identify material risk triggers and responses. Separate prerequisites for commitment from post-execution measurements; an unresolved prerequisite blocks only its dependent commitment.

Report unsupported commitments, demonstrated calculation errors and conditional recommendations distinctly. A reviewed planning document is not evidence that its initiative has succeeded. Use [document](document.md) for communication issues and the relevant design criteria for embedded technical or narrative mechanisms.
