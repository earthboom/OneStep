# Narrative design review

Assess premises, character arcs, relationship changes, world rules, event sequences and chapter outlines. Use the scoped [narrative design review](../../vegabond-fiction-design/references/review.md). Evaluate the proposed structure without treating unwritten scenes as existing evidence.

- Trace major decisions through each actor's goals, knowledge, means and consequences. Compare event chronology with disclosure order and reader access.
- Read consequential payoffs backward for prerequisites and the outline forward for their introduction. Follow subplot intersections and dispositions; preserve intentional openness where it serves the work.
- Check whether twists, emotional turns and resolutions follow established rules or require a new premise. Mark proposed additions as proposals; do not rescue a gap with invented canon.
- Evaluate interactions: more explanation may spoil suspense, faster pacing may lose emotional preparation, and closure may erase intended ambiguity. Do not require conflict, transformation or a formulaic structure in every work.

Locate findings by chapter/event/decision and explain the affected causal chain. Separate contradictions demonstrable in the outline from effects that must later be established in prose or assessed with readers. Suggest the earliest useful structural correction and identify affected downstream scenes; do not rewrite the outline without authorization.
