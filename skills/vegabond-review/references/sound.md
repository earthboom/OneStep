# Sound review

Establish the audio's role, intended playback context, duration/sync, source or wording to preserve and delivery requirements. Listen to the actual render for audible claims; distinguish listening, measurements and runtime/context checks.

- **Effects and ambience:** check cue recognition, attack/tail, unwanted clicks/noise, variation, layering and interaction with the intended soundscape.
- **BGM and adaptive music:** check musical function, transitions, loop seam over repeated playback, stem alignment and masking in context. Correct duration alone does not establish a seamless loop.
- **Voice:** check exact words, pronunciation, timing, intelligibility, breaths and processing at normal speed, including the target mix when available. A transcript does not prove audible clarity.
- **Songs:** assess hook, phrasing, vocal range/delivery, arrangement and lyric intelligibility against the brief. Separate taste and genre expectations from technical defects.
- **Delivery:** check the exact export's channels, sample rate, bit depth/encoding, duration, boundaries and relevant level/peak measurements against destination requirements. Do not impose a universal loudness target without a destination specification.

Read only relevant procedures from [vegabond-sound](../../vegabond-sound/SKILL.md) for deeper subtype checks; these do not authorize production. Cite real time ranges and listening context for audible findings. Metadata, a waveform, a score or production notes cannot establish how unavailable audio sounds. If listening is unavailable, return supported technical or brief findings and mark perceptual checks blocked; do not describe the render as heard.
