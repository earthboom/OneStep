# Fiction manuscript review

Read the actual prose within scope with its author direction, genre, viewpoint, story time and relevant canon. Apply the relevant sections of [manuscript review](../../vegabond-fiction/references/review.md), especially its uncertain-finding rules. Its correction steps apply only when revision is authorized.

- Trace consequential actions and emotional changes through goals, information, pressures and prior cues available at that point. Separate world facts, narrator claims, character beliefs and editorial inference.
- Check continuity, disclosure order, setup/payoff and viewpoint against precise passages. Do not invent unseen backstory to excuse a contradiction or demand exposition that spoils intentional uncertainty.
- Assess voice, dialogue, pacing, imagery and sentence choices by their scene function and the work's baseline. Intentional repetition, dialect, direct emotion or a steadfast character can be appropriate.
- Consider a text-supported alternative reading before calling a passage implausible or inconsistent. State what cue is missing when the available text cannot settle the concern.

Report passage/scene, evidence, intended reader effect and tradeoff. Separate demonstrated contradictions, interpretive concerns and optional preferences. AI agreement does not establish audience reaction or authorship. Missing chapters limit whole-work conclusions, not independent assessment of the supplied passage. Use [fiction-design](fiction-design.md) when the requested judgment concerns structural choices beyond the prose.
