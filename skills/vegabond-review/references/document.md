# Document review

Assess nonfiction against its audience, purpose, genre and requested medium. This domain covers general documents; the existing document production specialist is specifically for job applications.

- Check that the reader can locate the main point, understand the reasoning, and take the intended action. Match detail and terminology to their context.
- Trace consequential claims, quotations, numbers and recommendations to supplied evidence. Distinguish unsupported claims from demonstrated falsehoods. Reconcile dates, units, totals, references and repeated statements.
- Check omissions and ambiguity where they change interpretation or action. Preserve the author's meaning and voice; optional stylistic preferences are not factual defects.
- Inspect the actual final file or rendered pages when layout, tables, pagination, accessibility or export fidelity matters. Source text alone supports only a content review. Apply actual submission limits and their counting convention when known.

For applications, use [application writing](../../vegabond-document/references/job-application-writing.md) and the target posting/form. Verify role, contribution and outcomes without upgrading participation into leadership or inventing metrics. Other documents do not inherit hiring criteria.

Use [planning](planning.md) for feasibility or commitments within a proposal and [code-design](code-design.md) for technical mechanisms. Report exact passages and their consequence for the intended reader. Missing private or inaccessible sources remain evidence gaps; do not silently replace them with guesses.
