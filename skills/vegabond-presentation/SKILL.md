# Vegabond Presentation

Provide presentation methods for the active operation. Reuse its brief and task profile; follow the [agent instructions](../../agents/vegabond-agent.md) for common boundaries and completion. If invoked directly, use [scout's shared setup](../vegabond-scout/SKILL.md#frame-and-classify) and its linked operation skill, then continue here without restarting classification.

## Execute

Establish what the audience should understand or decide and how the presentation will be used. Inspect supplied sources, existing slides, template, and relevant speaker notes. Distinguish a requested outline or script from a finished editable deck. For artifact production or editing, load an available presentation skill suited to the requested format and follow its authoring and export procedure.

Use [vegabond-knowledge](../vegabond-knowledge/SKILL.md) for relevant audience, design/format conventions, source guidance, and prior content or rendering defects, reusing the active information brief.

- **Create:** Build a narrative from the audience's starting point to the intended conclusion. Assign each slide a purpose and supporting evidence, then choose text, diagrams, tables, or charts that communicate it. Build the requested artifact with consistent hierarchy and usable native elements where editing is required.
- **Revise:** Inspect the existing argument, order, and actual slide content before editing. Determine whether the problem lies in the evidence, story flow, wording, or visual arrangement. Preserve required theme, content, notes, and editability while repairing the affected layer.
- **Research:** Evaluate claims, audience needs, or presentation options using the relevant sources and slides. Cite findings and recommendations; leave the investigated deck unchanged.

## Verify

Check the narrative against the intended audience and conclusion. Verify material claims, citations, chart values, units, labels, and dates against sources; distinguish illustrative data from actual results.

Open or render the final delivered deck and inspect every slide for legibility, overflow, clipping, alignment, contrast, and ordering. Check the actual exported format for slide count, required editable content, and requested notes or interactive features. A source preview alone does not establish that the export is usable; identify which viewer or renderer was used and any untested application-specific behavior.

## Correct

Fix unsupported claims or a broken narrative before polishing their layout. For visual defects, adjust hierarchy, spacing, chart design, or content density while retaining the intended meaning. Inspect the corrected export, recheck affected transitions and references, and visually inspect every slide of the final deck. Return the artifact or requested findings with content evidence, visual inspection results, and remaining compatibility limitations.

Supply observed content or visual problems, corrections, final-export evidence, and reusable checks to vegabond-knowledge for authorized recording and recurrence prevention.
