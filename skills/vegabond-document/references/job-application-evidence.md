# Evidence for writing job applications

Source review dated 2026-11-24. Read the posting and its attachments for binding submission instructions. Use this review to decide which writing or checking methods to carry into a particular application.

## What the studies measured

| Study and design | Finding and limit | Application to the skill |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Wiles, Munyikwa and Horton (2025), online labor market field experiment with nearly half a million job seekers and nongenerative writing assistance. [Management Science](https://doi.org/10.1287/mnsc.2024.04528) | Treated applicants were hired 8% more often relative to the control group. Their résumé text had fewer errors and was easier to read. This intervention and market limit the applicability of the estimate. | Edit for clarity and accuracy while preserving factual content. Use the estimate only when discussing that study. |
| Bright and Hutton (2000), 62 managers and HR consultants rated genuine résumés with experimentally varied competency statements. [International Journal of Selection and Assessment](https://doi.org/10.1111/1468-2389.00132) | Competency statements raised ratings in the study. Their placement did not change ratings. The endpoint was the raters' assessment. | Make relevant competencies explicit through evidence. Treat section ordering as a reading and form decision. |
| Cole, Rubin, Feild and Giles (2007), 244 recruiters evaluated résumés from 122 recent or soon-to-be graduates. [Applied Psychology](https://doi.org/10.1111/j.1464-0597.2007.00288.x) | Perceived academic qualifications, work experience and extracurricular activities interacted in predicting perceived employability in this sample. | Consider projects, coursework and activities as possible evidence for early-career applicants. Keep the type of experience precise and follow the target's rules. |
| Abbas Nejad, Musillo, Wicker and Zaccaria, two preregistered cover-letter field experiments. [Tilburg University research record](https://research.tilburguniversity.edu/en/publications/labor-market-signals-the-role-of-large-language-models-2/), [author manuscript, Table 1 and Appendix Table A3](https://twicker97.github.io/JM_documents/ChatGPT.pdf) | ChatGPT access raised mean cover-letter scores, mainly in standardized opening and closing sections. The study did not detect an improvement in motivation or in recommendations to interview. Motivation and perceived clarity were important predictors of recommendations in that setting. | Ask for the applicant's actual reasons and what makes this opening relevant. Use editing help to make those reasons easier to understand and check. |
| Cui, Dias Santamarina and Ye (2026), difference-in-differences study of a Freelancer.com cover-letter tool rollout. [Yale Cowles discussion paper, Tables 3 and 5 and §5](https://cowles.yale.edu/sites/default/files/2026-11/d2577.pdf) | Tool access increased text-to-post alignment. The association between tailoring and callbacks fell by 52% after rollout, while employers gave more weight to work histories. The estimated gain in callbacks from access was 0.38 percentage points in one main specification and was sensitive to the parallel-trends assumption. | Use the posting to select genuine work evidence. Check the visibility of that evidence independently of how closely wording matches the ad. |

The studies measure different outcomes. Readability, résumé ratings, interview recommendations, marketplace callbacks and hires answer different questions. None of these studies directly compares the proposed workflow with alternatives in Korean recruiting. The link from the research to the skill is an editorial inference and should be evaluated on real documents.

## Korean practice and formal requirements

- **Work24.** The résumé guide describes standard and competency-based fields. The self-introduction guides connect role experience, motivation and goals to posting requirements, and describe STAR as a way to organize a behavioral example. Their content helps decide what facts to gather and which questions the text must answer. A specific employer's form governs which fields to fill and what personal material is allowed. [Résumé guide](https://www.work24.go.kr/wk/r/d/1110/resumeSelfIntroGuide1.do), [experience, motivation and goals](https://www.work24.go.kr/wk/r/d/1111/resumeSelfIntroGuide4.do), [final review and STAR](https://www.work24.go.kr/wk/r/d/1111/resumeSelfIntroGuide5.do)
- **NCS.** Its public application example distinguishes paid 경력 from unpaid 경험 and asks for the person's actions, role and results. Use this distinction when the target form uses it. Verify how the employer handles internship, freelance, part-time and overlapping periods before computing eligible service. [NCS application example](https://m.ncs.go.kr/mobile/rm02/RH10300302.do)
- **Ministry of Employment and Labor.** The 2025 guidebook describes fair recruitment practices. A separate ministry-affiliated hiring form shows how required administrative fields and blind free-text rules can coexist. The latter is an archived example. Inspect the actual posting for exceptions, acceptable supporting evidence and which fields its restrictions cover. [Fair recruitment guidebook](https://www.moel.go.kr/local/bucheon/info/policydata/view.do?bbs_seq=20250301868), [MOEL Customer Counseling Center application, Forms 1–2](https://1350.moel.go.kr/home/hp/assets/file/notice/notice_20250527_1013_1_1.pdf)
- **Ministry of Patriots and Veterans Affairs.** Its career-statement guide calls for relevant project experience, how the work was done, the individual's role, and supporting outcomes. The audience is transitioning veterans. Adopt the emphasis on role and method, and let the applicable posting and evidence determine length, attachments and metrics. [Career statement guide](https://vnet.go.kr/em/EmJal003.do)

## Writing techniques and training

- **Résumé accomplishments.** MIT describes Project–Activity–Result (PAR) and distinguishes ways to quantify the scale of work. Harvard provides targeted résumé and cover-letter examples. Use a short action–scope–result or action–deliverable bullet when the evidence supports it. Keep the full story in working notes or in an experience statement where the format allows it. [MIT Career Advising & Professional Development](https://capd.mit.edu/resources/resumes-writing-about-your-skills/), [Harvard Mignone Center](https://careerservices.fas.harvard.edu/resources/create-a-strong-resume/)
- **Motivation and permitted AI help.** Work24 distinguishes job and employer motivation. Harvard's AI guide asks applicants to check employer instructions and use distinctive organizational facts tied to their experience. Establish the employer fact and the applicant's own reason independently. Their connection is the applicant's argument. [Work24 motivation guide](https://www.work24.go.kr/wk/r/d/1111/resumeSelfIntroGuide4.do), [Harvard AI guidance](https://careerservices.fas.harvard.edu/ai-resumes-and-cover-letters/)
- **Korean workshop.** The Suwon Job Center lecture's chapter list locates its motivation and strengths-and-weaknesses section at 45:37. Use it as training for comparing the purpose of each answer. The chapter description was available for this review. The full recording and transcript were unavailable. [Suwon workshop, 45:37](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Sb9eELkOUpA&t=2737s)
- **English workshops.** Tufts outlines a short video covering résumé purpose, organization, accomplishments and tailoring. UC Riverside pairs a technical application recording with slides. The slides' data-science example develops a cover-letter argument from a specific posting and expands one relevant experience in its body (PDF pages 31–37). The listed topics and slides were inspected. These are teaching sources. Treat scanning-time claims and preferred layouts as heuristics. [Tufts Career Essentials](https://careers.tufts.edu/channels/resumes/#career-essentials-resume-video-workshop), [UC Riverside recording](https://youtu.be/FDk4rCsyGM0), [UC Riverside slides](https://gradcareers.ucr.edu/sites/default/files/2024-09/2024-25_technical_resume_cover_letter_writing.pdf)

## Technical checks and unsupported generalizations

Greenhouse documents parsing failures with certain columns, tables, graphics and text in headers, footers or text boxes. Lever documents file types its parser accepts and suggests checking that text can be selected. These sources describe specific products and versions. Let the employer's instructions choose the format. For an unrestricted, machine-parsed résumé, start with simple selectable text and inspect the final export and its extraction order. If the live portal provides an authorized preview, compare its actual auto-filled fields with the original. [Greenhouse parsing documentation](https://support.greenhouse.io/hc/en-us/articles/200989175-Unsuccessful-resume-parse), [Lever parsing documentation](https://help.lever.co/s/article/Understanding-Resume-Parsing)

Local text extraction, portal parsing, ranking and rejection are different events. These sources do not substantiate a universal ATS score, a required keyword density, a fixed recruiter reading time, or a format that wins across employers. STAR, achievement bullets and straightforward layouts are writing and inspection methods. Acceptance depends on the particular submission and the evidence it contains.
