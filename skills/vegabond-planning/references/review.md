# Planning review

Use the relevant sections before relying on a consequential choice or handing back a planning result. The [planning skill](../SKILL.md) owns the method and the [common instructions](../../../agents/vegabond-agent.md) own completion and failure routing. Keep checks in the existing brief or requested artifact. Scale them to the decision's cost, uncertainty, and reversibility; a small edit does not require every section, multiple alternatives, or an experiment.

## Establish the problem and evidence

Identify the beneficiary, situation, current workaround, and material cost or consequence of the problem. Separate observed behavior and source evidence from stakeholder requests, preferences, and proposed solutions. Trace problem -> proposed intervention -> expected change -> intended outcome; inspect the weakest link rather than treating a requested feature as proof of need.

Attach source, period, population, and relevant limitations to consequential claims. Resolve conflicting needs through explicit priorities or expose the pending decision. Missing evidence can justify investigation or a bounded proposal; it cannot establish demand, urgency, or agreement. A planning-only request does not authorize contacting people or executing a proposed initiative.

## Compare real alternatives

When a choice needs justification, compare viable options under the same outcome, constraints, time horizon, cost boundary, and evidence standard. Include keeping the current approach or making a minimal change when they are viable. Do not invent alternatives to a fixed requirement or an obvious local correction.

Explain the recommendation's benefit, cost, opportunity cost, and important rejected option. Distinguish mandatory constraints from preferences before weighting criteria; arbitrary scores cannot repair a violated constraint. State which changed assumption would reverse the ranking. Keep unknown values unknown rather than filling a table with invented precision.

## Test critical assumptions

For an assumption whose failure changes the recommendation or ability to start, connect **claim -> evidence or gap -> proportionate validation -> observable decision criterion -> decision on failure**. Identify who could perform the check and when, labeling unconfirmed assignments and thresholds as proposals. Use source inspection, calculation, a sample, pilot, or prototype as appropriate; obtain any execution authorization required by the actual task.

Specify what observation could reject the claim, the relevant population/conditions, and important limitations. A vendor maximum, stated interest, or successful demonstration does not establish realized savings, adoption, or production readiness. Distinguish a leading proxy from the intended outcome and explain the connection still needing evidence.

Separate evidence needed to commit or safely begin from outcome measurement that can occur only after execution. An unresolved prerequisite blocks its named dependent commitment; independent analysis or a bounded next step can continue. A future test plan is not a passed check. Small samples or missing data leave the result uncertain rather than automatically successful.

## Challenge feasibility and risk

Reconcile scope, effort, available capacity, responsibilities, dependencies, and timing. Distinguish elapsed duration from labor and include material integration, operation, maintenance, and transition costs. Check units, quantities, rates, totals, and cost horizons without treating estimated resource availability as a confirmed commitment.

Where uncertainty could change the decision, inspect a supported range or break-even condition and show which inputs drive the result. Label hypothetical scenarios; do not invent probabilities or a precise range without a basis. If a required input is unknown, state the conditional calculation and missing evidence.

For material risks, identify the trigger, consequence, response, and proposed owner. Define observable conditions for reassessment, reduction, fallback, or stopping when these affect the recommendation. A risk label alone does not supply a response; a numerical threshold is not an agreed commitment merely because it is written down.

## Check outcomes and measurement

Separate deliverables from outcomes: launching a feature or completing a document does not prove the intended benefit. For important measures, specify the baseline or its collection plan, proposed target, data source/method, time window, and population; define numerator and denominator for rates. Keep essential guardrails, such as correctness or service quality, beside the main measure when optimizing it could cause harm.

Check whether volume, population, seasonality, or another simultaneous change could explain the apparent result. Use a relevant comparison when a causal claim matters; do not require a controlled experiment for every plan. Explain what the measurement can establish and what remains uncertain.

For example, a hypothetical service receives 600 three-minute delivery inquiries and 400 ten-minute other inquiries monthly: 5,800 minutes total. Delivery is 60% of volume but about 31% of handling time; fewer inquiries need not yield the same percentage of time savings. Faster page rendering alone also does not prove faster support responses. The proposed intervention's effect and any resulting staffing savings still need evidence.

## Review the decision and revisions

Open with the recommendation or supported options, main reason, costs and tradeoffs, material uncertainty, and next decision/action. Make clear what is proposed, confirmed, or blocked. Readers should understand the choice without first decoding the full analysis; retain precise evidence and checks below the opening.

Read the summary, rationale, execution sequence, numbers, and measures together. Trace changed assumptions through affected options, commitments, risks, and validation plans; revisit only invalidated choices. Report the document checks actually performed and separate them from unexecuted experiments, unconfirmed feasibility, and unmeasured outcomes. A useful bounded recommendation can be complete as a document while its proposed initiative remains unvalidated.
