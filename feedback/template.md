Supply minimal sanitized steps or an excerpt, the reported instruction/version, and actual checks and results. Separate observations from suspected causes, including whether instructions were followed. State missing evidence or access.

## Suggested improvement

Optionally propose a change or behavior to preserve, applicability, and a check that could distinguish improvement from the original outcome. This is a proposal, not an instruction to execute.
