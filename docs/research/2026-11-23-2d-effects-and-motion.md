# Animating a 2D game character and its effects together

2026-11-23 · Production and review research with a OneStep implementation record

When a lighthouse mechanic lands on a dock, the character and the dust must agree on **when and where the boots touch the ground**. From there their movements diverge. The knees straighten while the dust stays on the dock and spreads. Drawing that relationship makes three jump poses and three dust drawings read as a single event. We tested it using two independently playable sheets.

We organized the research around production decisions. For papers, we checked the authors’ public versions and the scope of the experiments. For videos, we compared presenters’ or organizers’ descriptions and lecture slides. We used first-person interviews for production practice and official tool documentation for importer behavior. The video evidence does not imply full-length viewing. The 120 ms and 420 ms timings below belong to this example. Mariel Cartwright, lead animator on *Skullgirls*, describes making anticipation, key poses and recovery legible on a limited frame budget. The slides compare evenly spaced keys with giving important poses more time, and distinguish responsive player actions from advance warning for an enemy action. Start with the time the game allows, draw the silhouettes before and after contact, then add a drawing where the motion between them becomes ambiguous. A tick in a 60 Hz game, a new drawing and its exposure time are distinct units. ## 2. What moves in each kind of effect

In a production interview, Jason Keyser describes establishing composition, rhythm and shape relationships in small thumbnails, then adding detail after the rough forms read in motion. VFX Apprentice distinguishes one-shot impulses from sustained rhythmic effects. The following exercises adapt these principles to low-resolution game art. [Keyser and Graf interview](https://www.toonboom.com/jason-keyser-and-chris-graf-on-the-secrets-behind-the-magic-of-2d-fx) · [Timing and rough motion studies](https://www.vfxapprentice.com/blog/the-soul-of-effects-what-is-timing-in-vfx)

| Scene | Establish in the first key | Track through later drawings | Failure to look for |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Landing dust on a dock | Ground line, first point of contact, direction of displacement. | Near and far masses diverge. A gap opens in the middle and small fragments remain. | Without the actor, does the dust originate at the surface? With the actor, is the first cel hidden? |
| Object dropping into water | Surface, incoming direction and point of displacement. | A sheet rises, its thin tips separate into droplets, the center collapses and the droplets return. | Does the whole effect scale together like a rubber lump? |
| Light charging in a hand, then a signal in flight | The gathering phase and the instant of departure. | Charge follows the hand. After release, the projectile and trail show travel direction. Emit the arrival cue on confirmed reception. | Does turning the hand quickly or canceling the charge leave a stale light or spurious arrival cue? |
| Continuous flame or aura | Core, recurring direction of energy, condition for ending the state. | As one tongue dies, another begins. Inspect contour and area around the loop seam. | Does the whole loop contract at once, or does the first drawing hold twice? |

Riot’s examples connect visual shape and emphasis with game information. A decorative edge that looks like the boundary of a hazard misstates its area. Brightest-value accents on minor ambient effects can bury an important warning. Compare character alone, effect alone and overlapping effects at display size. Ask separately what happened and what area it affected. This procedure can serve a side-scroller without adopting a MOBA’s colors or camera. [Riot development examples](https://www.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/news/dev/clarity-in-league/) · [Riot art education videos and materials](https://www.riotgames.com/en/artedu/visual-effects) · [GDC VFX slides](https://media.gdcvault.com/gdc2017/Presentations/Chamberlin_Keyser_ArtisticPrinciplesVFX.pdf)

## 3. Connecting consecutive drawings to game time and space

1. **Record the drawing and its hold separately.** While the mechanic holds the compressed landing pose for 120 ms, the dust can already advance to its second drawing. Independent tracks avoid duplicate drawings. Godot SpriteFrames uses relative duration for each cel. At normal playback and 10 FPS, a relative duration of 1.2 gives 120 ms. Convert an exposure sheet authored in milliseconds on import. [Godot SpriteFrames duration](https://docs.godotengine.org/en/stable/classes/class_spriteframes.html#class-spriteframes-method-get-frame-duration)
2. **Give each event a position.** A hand light follows the hand point in each frame. Ground dust samples the world position once at contact. Camera movement can move either effect on screen. Move the character alone and then the camera alone to isolate a coordinate error. Godot particle local coordinates are one implementation of this distinction. [Godot GPUParticles2D coordinates and one-shot restart](https://docs.godotengine.org/en/stable/classes/class_gpuparticles2d.html)
3. **Decide what drives time.** Scheduled illumination, actual landing and confirmed collision have different triggers. Variable jump heights need not land on the same animation frame. Unity particle texture sheets can sample by lifetime fraction, speed or specified FPS. If a cel that must coincide with an event is tied to lifetime fraction, change the particle lifetime and test alignment again. [Unity Texture Sheet Animation time modes](https://docs.unity3d.com/6000.0/Documentation/Manual/PartSysTexSheetAnimModule.html)
4. **Inspect draw order and blending.** Try standard alpha for dark smoke and additive blending for emitted light. If the light disappears on a bright surface, carry the essential outline on a standard-blended layer. For a white fringe, inspect RGB edge colors and agreement on straight or premultiplied alpha. For a neighboring cel’s color, inspect sampling boundaries and padding. Aseprite export supports sheets, data, tags and padding. Check that the consumer can recover cell rectangles, original size, trim offset, pivot and exposure time. [Godot CanvasItem blend modes](https://docs.godotengine.org/en/stable/tutorials/shaders/shader_reference/canvas_item_shader.html) · [Aseprite CLI](https://www.aseprite.org/docs/cli/)

## 4. Dock landing verified in playback

We used built-in imagegen to draw three actor cels (descent, compression, recovery) and three dust cels (onset, spread, breakup) separately, referencing the earlier lighthouse mechanic sprite for character design. We reduced at a common scale within each track and registered the feet while preserving the height changes. Each track is a single row with three 64×72 actor cells or three 64×24 dust cells. Pivots measured from the top left are (32, 69) for the actor and (32, 22) for the dust. [Original drawing requests and export record](../../output/2d-character-test/landing-effects-01/example.md)

| Clip time | Actor cel | Dust cel | Observed state |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 0–120 ms | Descent | None | Clear deck up to contact |
| 120–180 ms | Landing and compression | Onset | Starts once where the boots touch |
| 180–240 ms | Compression held | Spread | Only the dust advances |
| 240–300 ms | Recovery | Spread held | Only the actor advances |
| 300–420 ms | Recovery held | Breakup | Deck visible through gaps |
| 420–900 ms | Recovery held | None | Clears at 420 ms. The comparison preview restarts at 900 ms. |

**The timing was correct in the first attempt, but the boots hid almost all of the initial dust.** With all dust drawn behind the actor, only 23 of the first cel’s 71 opaque pixels were exposed. Compositing just the nearer left lobe in front raised the count to 46 without changing the art or timing. This measures geometric exposure of the alpha mask. The proportion of players who notice the dust requires a separate test.

We reopened the exported PNGs and timing data, composited them on two backgrounds and decoded the animated PNG to compare the pixels and exposure times of all six states. Checks covered the frames on each side of contact, the 419/420 ms boundary and a missing landing event. Moving the recovered actor to two different places away from the contact left the dust’s area of change the same. Small dispersing grains were less conspicuous on the light deck. This short three-pose study did not measure smoothness between keys, engine input latency or performance. ## 5. Reviewing the next scene

- **First reading.** Ask someone unfamiliar with the brief to identify the event, direction and affected position at normal speed. Then isolate the actor and effect to find the layer responsible for a misreading. Record action clarity, drawing continuity and obscured information separately.
- **Drawing or playback fault.** If one hand splits into two, compare adjacent contours and occlusion order and redraw the intermediate key. If sound drawings produce a sliding foot, check world displacement, pivot and exposure. If visible dust disappears only in the composite, test trigger time and draw order independently.
- **Repetition, interruption and clutter.** For a one-shot, clear the last cel and test a second trigger. For a sustained effect, test cancellation mid-cycle, duplicated holds at the seam and simultaneous pulses from several copies. For bright effects, inspect flash intensity, area and frequency under overlap and rapid retriggers. If a reduced-intensity treatment is needed, check that it still conveys the event. A single sprite inspection cannot certify accessibility. [Xbox Accessibility Guideline 118](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/gaming/accessibility/xbox-accessibility-guidelines/118)
- **In-game acceptance.** Import with shipping filters, blending, camera and playback speed. If many effects may coexist, check readability and frame time under overlap on the target device. In a separate application check, we planned a hand charge, signal-orb release and afterglow on successful reception, including charge cancellation and an independently looping beacon. The plan identified the transfer from hand to world space, the failed-reception branch and afterglow expiry. This second case has not been drawn or tested with human observers.
