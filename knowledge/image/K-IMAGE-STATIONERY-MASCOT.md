# Will a mascot's identity and reaction read on the finished stationery?

## Guidance

Design around one observable habit and a few landmarks such as body contour, face position and a mark on the character's anatomical left. For an original mascot inspired by another property, translate the requested mood into gesture and shape and give the new design its own identity. Existing characters can use the supplied design or authorized model sheet. A quiet belly can serve as writing space on a die-cut memo, while a chat sticker needs an instantly readable gesture. For a series, derive each pose from a neutral design or share the body and mark layers for flat same-view expressions. A turn changes which surfaces and marks can be seen. Sketch the new view first or create another master. Give each variant one everyday event and a readable response. A cast lineup, pose sheet or turn sheet earns its cost when it answers a particular view or relationship question. ## Applicability and exceptions

For a series of illustrated scenes, StoryDiffusion's demonstrations show what shared attention can produce across images. Before choosing it over separate edits, compare identity and new poses at the actual sticker size and record whether individual cells can be repaired economically. The demonstrations cover generated scenes; print proofing requires a separate check. [StoryDiffusion](https://storydiffusion.github.io/)

Use text-only candidates for exploration, individual reference edits for separately repairable assets, and a one-pass sheet for comparing a set; inspect every cell before reuse. Spatial conditioning may help a difficult pose; shared attention or subject fine-tuning needs pipeline support and a comparison on unseen views. Verify permission to use training or publication assets. Methods trained on photos or evaluated by aggregate similarity can miss a mirrored notch. Research on infant faces, animal pictures and curved shapes offers variables to test without establishing a universal cuteness formula or buying preference. ## Checks

- Ask a reviewer to name the first emotion or action at actual delivery size without the caption. Record identity, readability and preference separately. Decode alpha and inspect the entire silhouette over light and dark backgrounds; transparent corners can coexist with edge residue. Check text and the selected marketplace's current submission and AI rules. [PNG standard](https://www.w3.org/TR/png/) · [LINE static stickers](https://creator.line.me/en/guideline/sticker/) · [LINE terms](https://creator.line.me/en/terms/) · [Kakao operating principles](https://emoticonstudio.kakao.com/guideline)
- For a printed sticker, obtain the printer's template and check physical cut size, effective PPI, profile, bleed, safety margin, closed cut paths and gutter spacing. A printed border and a cut path serve different jobs. A physical-size proof is needed for print acceptance; vendors specify different margins. Repeat the tile at least three times at actual tape width to expose seams, uneven gaps and reversed motifs. ## Evidence

A book-cart trial compared text-only generation, a neutral image reference and shared layers on three front-view reactions and a held-out turn. Reference edits retained the body and tab in all four. Every “thank you” read initially as holding or offering an object. The 113 px comparison approximated 30 mm at 96 ppi. It used one first result per condition and blind AI readings, with no physical proof, representative customer ratings or platform submission.
