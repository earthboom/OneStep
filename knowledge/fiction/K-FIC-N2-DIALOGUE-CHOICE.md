# Retain the author-selected single reassurance in novel-2 reunion editing

## Guidance

In the 003 reunion preserve the selected line 이렇게 무사히 돌아왔으니 됐다……. Do not restore 고생 많았다 merely to retain an earlier emotional intent. After a rejected same-speaker merge, reassess the scene's central response and what the author actually asked to preserve before adding connective wording.

## Applicability and exceptions

This is the author's specific choice for novel-2, not a ban on acknowledging hardship or on multiple emotional statements elsewhere. Author directions and the existing style guide own preferences. Distinct speakers, meaningful pauses, necessary direct emotion, and newly authorized dialogue edits require contextual judgment. This entry is a scoped retrieval aid, not a new canon owner or style system.

## Checks

Identify who speaks, whether intervening action justifies a new paragraph, which meaning advances the scene, and whether the author explicitly removed a phrase. Compare actual variants after rejected edits rather than preserving every sentence mechanically. Separate author preference changes from demonstrated defects and do not infer majority reader agreement.
