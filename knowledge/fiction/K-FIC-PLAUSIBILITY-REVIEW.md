# Judge narrative plausibility with scoped claims and checkable textual evidence

## Guidance

Use within [manuscript review](../../skills/vegabond-fiction/references/review.md) or design review. [Research and source limits](K-FIC-RESEARCH-PLAUSIBILITY.md) own the evidence base. The following is this project's editorial synthesis, not an implementation of TRIP, IPOCL or ConStory-Checker.

### Establish what may be judged

Read the supplied span and consequential context. Identify revision, story time, genre/premise, narrator, viewpoint, author constraints and missing chapters. A fantastic action may establish a premise; demand only the cues needed for this decision, not an encyclopedic rule explanation. An excerpt cannot certify whole-novel consistency.

For a disputed claim retain its entity, predicate/state, time, world/branch, source and epistemic status. Separate authoritative events from dialogue, lies, beliefs, memories, guesses, metaphors and hypothetical statements. Put reader knowledge and each relevant character's accessible information in separate columns when they differ.

### Inspect the consequential transition

Use only the needed part of this state sketch:

`before/condition → actor's information, belief and aim → action/cost → after/consequence`

Locate the text establishing possession, position, time, capability, world rule or other relevant precondition. Does a later event require a condition the text explicitly denies? Did an intervening action change it? Distinguish sequence from cause. For character choice, ask what the actor is trying to protect or obtain under their belief and pressure. A failed plan, expensive kindness, panic or bad judgment is not automatically a plot contradiction. Do not invent a trauma, secret power or unseen message to excuse it.

### Make the finding auditable

For each material concern, keep this compact chain in the existing review:

| Field | Required content for that finding |
| --- | --- |
| Target and location | Actual assertion/action and its text locator. |
| Governing condition | Rule, prior state or expectation with a separate locator; mark explicit fact versus defeasible inference. |
| Mechanism | Briefly show what cannot jointly hold, what precondition fails, or what necessary link is not yet supported. |
| Best supported alternative | Check different time/world, changed state, information access, deception, unreliable narration or deliberate omission when supported in this text. State any added assumption an explanation would require. |
| Verdict and uncertainty | Demonstrated error; unresolved/insufficient evidence; or no demonstrated error. Keep strengths and distinguish an optional clarity preference. Confidence follows evidence quality, not a fabricated percentage. |
| Next action | Smallest warranted repair or focused question; expected benefit, preservation/tradeoff and dependent recheck. No-error findings need no repair. |

A demonstrated contradiction needs incompatible claims/conditions in the same relevant frame. Missing narration alone is not such evidence. A missing essential explanation can still be a reader-understanding concern: report the unsupported link and uncertainty instead of silently declaring impossible action or inventing its cause. Possible omission is not proof the author intended it.

### Calibrate the critic

For factual/quantitative constraints, use appropriate primary sources or simple calculations when the claim requires them; do not replace historical, technical or cultural evidence with model familiarity. Mark externally unverified claims. Mechanical checks can verify dates, literal counts and locations under explicit assumptions; natural-language extraction and the assumptions still need review.

For long works, inspect linked earlier/later evidence and unresolved findings, not only the most recent summary or retrieved top hit. Reopen a judgment when context changes. For consequential alternative drafts, preserve versions and blind or vary comparison order when useful. Agreement among AI critics is additional analysis, not a reader study or independent truth. If no new evidence arrives, repeatedly asking for more flaws is not improved verification.

## Applicability and exceptions

Do not conflate physical realism, internal consistency, character motivation, morality, probability and artistic success. A rare coincidence needs scene-specific evaluation; no unsupported numerical probability or universal coincidence quota is supplied. A purposeful choice need not be efficient, successful, wise or kind. A social prohibition is not a physical impossibility; its consequences depend on institutions, enforcement and context.

Preserve supported mystery, dream logic, unreliable narration, evolving relationships and purposeful uncertainty. Conversely, a genre label cannot repair a direct contradiction of an established exceptionless rule. Check the strongest text-supported alternative before either condemning or excusing the passage.

## Checks

- Can another reviewer verify the decisive quotation and locator in the supplied version?
- Are both sides of a contradiction in the same time, world and assertion layer?
- Are unknown, unsupported and explicitly false states kept distinct?
- Does the action follow a text-supported aim/belief rather than the reviewer's ideal decision?
- Was the relevant alternative examined without inventing support?
- Does the classification justify the proposed repair, while retaining useful voice and ambiguity?
- After any repair, were affected state, information and later consequences rechecked?
