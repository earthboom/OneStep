# Evaluate character perspective without equating empathy and virtue

## Guidance

P06 proposes several dimensions of identification and linguistic cues, with causal propositions still requiring tests. P07's meta-analyses and P08's replications limit claims about automatic social-cognitive improvement from reading.

As an editorial application, inspect a consequential choice through what this person notices, believes, wants, can do and risks losing. Cite the passage giving access to that perspective, then check what the action changes for another person. Understanding a harmful choice need not mean liking or endorsing it. A morally good character is not automatically compelling; cruelty is not automatically complexity.

For a relationship, inspect both directions: what A expects from B, what B believes about A, what each withholds and what an actual exchange changes. Dialogue should show a tactic or response grounded in their distinct knowledge and aims. Use the existing character and relationship entries rather than inventing a compulsory sympathy checklist.

## Applicability and exceptions

A distant narrator can withhold mental access deliberately. A steadfast character need not undergo a conversion. Unreliable thought, contradiction and irrationality can be characterization when the text supports them. Do not demand trauma, redemption or a likable trait from every antagonist.

P07 reports small average effects under particular outcomes and controls; P08 found no immediate advantage in its four replications. They address different evidence levels and do not prove either that fiction has no value or that a particular manuscript makes readers better people. Empathy-task performance is not ethical behavior.

## Checks

- Does a major turn follow from available information, pressure and established disposition?
- Is the perspective actually present or supplied by the reviewer?
- Are relationship changes shown through choices and responses?
- Can a text-supported alternative explain apparently inconsistent behavior?
- Is a proposed likability edit necessary for this work, or merely taste?
