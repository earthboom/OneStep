# Select research on narrative experience with evidence limits attached

## Guidance

Use with the [craft guide](K-FIC-CRAFT-GUIDE.md), not as its replacement. Start from the current work's intended readers, genre, voice and scene problem. Retrieve a relevant topic and inspect its evidence boundary before applying it. The [evaluation method](K-FIC-EVALUATION.md) connects these references to actual passages, competing interpretations and revision checks.

Use empirical results for the question actually studied. A recall task does not measure enjoyment; a self-report scale does not directly grade a manuscript; a film result is not automatically a novel result; a survey path is not a causal manipulation. Track the studied outcome before choosing a craft intervention.

## Applicability and exceptions

The [plausibility and AI-judgment expansion](K-FIC-RESEARCH-PLAUSIBILITY.md), checked 2026-11-17, adds ten separately inspected papers from 2008-2026 and a [scoped review method](K-FIC-PLAUSIBILITY-REVIEW.md). It reuses P01 without recounting or redating that source check. The original sixteen-paper ledger retains its dates and access depths. Use the expansion for internal consistency, intentional action, character information and critic reliability; benchmark results are not literary-quality scores.

This is a purposive multidisciplinary expansion, not an exhaustive or preregistered literature review. Search clusters covered situation models, absorption/transportation, suspense and spoilers, character identification and empathy, literary style, appreciation/negative emotion, Korean web-novel audiences and AI-assisted creativity. Searches used English and Korean terms through the connected web search service. Preferred publisher texts, author university repositories and author abstracts indexed in PubMed/KCI. Included claims require inspected source text; search snippets alone are not inclusion evidence.

The sixteen-paper ledger includes one paper already present as S20 in the craft collection (P04); it adds fifteen distinct scholarly references rather than sixteen wholly new ones. A meta-analysis contains overlapping primary studies: do not count it and its component papers as independent confirmations. Abstract-only inspection supports the reported high-level result, not a full audit of measures, exclusions, bias or statistical robustness. No corrections/retraction registry or comprehensive citation-chain review was performed.

Most evidence concerns short materials, particular languages, student samples or audiovisual media. Korean prose, serial platforms, expert authors, genre communities and complete long novels require work-specific checks. A source's appearance in this ledger does not certify generality or the efficacy of a derived writing technique. Theories remain theories even when published in a peer-reviewed journal.

## Checks

- Record the source ID, date, type, inspected locator, studied task/outcome and important limit with a consequential use.
- Distinguish the paper's result, this collection's editorial adaptation, and the current manuscript's evidence.
- Preserve uncertainty, alternative readings and counterevidence. Never infer ethical worth, sales or literary value from an empathy task or immersion score.
- Evaluate the actual draft separately. Use a real reader report only when one actually exists; no simulated panel establishes reader consensus.
- Verify retrieval, links and translations independently of claims about the quality of fiction.

## Evidence

Checked on 2026-11-17, Asia/Seoul. User requested broad research, including scholarly papers, to strengthen fiction evaluation and writing toward enjoyment and meaning. Source summaries below are deliberately brief; no full articles or questionnaire items are reproduced. P labels are local to this research ledger, separate from S labels in the craft guide.

### P01

[Zwaan, Langston & Graesser (1995), The Construction of Situation Models in Narrative Comprehension: An Event-Indexing Model](https://www.psychologicalscience.org/journals/psychological-science/j.1467-9280.1995.tb00513.x/). DOI: 10.1111/j.1467-9280.1995.tb00513.x. APS author abstract inspected. Model tested with a verb-clustering task: events connected through time, space, protagonist, causality and intentionality. Supports a comprehension lens; not a study of whole-novel appeal.

### P02

[Green & Brock (2000), The role of transportation in the persuasiveness of public narratives](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/11079236/). DOI: 10.1037/0022-3514.79.5.701. PubMed author abstract inspected: four experiments connect transportation, story-consistent beliefs and protagonist evaluations. Persuasion is the studied outcome; immersion does not establish truth or ethical desirability.

### P03

[Kuijpers, Hakemulder, Tan & Doicaru (2014), Exploring absorbing reading experiences](https://research-portal.uu.nl/en/publications/exploring-absorbing-reading-experiences-developing-and-validating/). DOI: 10.1075/ssol.4.1.05kui. University author abstract inspected; textual-narrative scale development using interviews, pilot work and factor analysis. Attention, transportation, emotional engagement and imagery are distinguished. Full scale and validation statistics not inspected; Korean adaptations are not thereby validated.

### P04

[Lehne & Koelsch (2015), Toward a general psychological model of tension and suspense](https://www.frontiersin.org/journals/psychology/articles/10.3389/fpsyg.2015.00079/full). DOI: 10.3389/fpsyg.2015.00079. Publisher abstract and introductory definition inspected. Theoretical synthesis linking consequential anticipation and unresolved states; not a new controlled writing experiment. Same source as craft S20, not independent corroboration.

### P05

[Delatorre et al. (2018), Confronting a Paradox](https://www.frontiersin.org/journals/psychology/articles/10.3389/fpsyg.2018.01392/full). DOI: 10.3389/fpsyg.2018.01392. Publisher experiment, participants, method and discussion inspected. Sixty-eight Spanish undergraduates read short variants twice and rated suspense. Outcome uncertainty and rereading show different patterns. Immediate repeated exposure, narrow stimuli and reported suspense limit transfer; do not infer that spoilers always help.

### P06

[van Krieken, Hoeken & Sanders (2017), Evoking and Measuring Identification with Narrative Characters](https://www.frontiersin.org/journals/psychology/articles/10.3389/fpsyg.2017.01190/full). DOI: 10.3389/fpsyg.2017.01190. Publisher abstract, framework and discussion inspected. Six proposed perspective/identification dimensions connect linguistic cues to reader processes. Hypothesis-and-theory article with a research agenda; six experimentally proven writing rules are not claimed.

### P07

[Wimmer et al. (2024), Cognitive effects and correlates of reading fiction](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/38602788/). DOI: 10.1037/xge0001583. Published author abstract inspected: preregistered meta-analyses report 70 experiments, g = 0.14, CI [0.06, 0.21], and 114 correlational studies, r = .16, CI [0.13, 0.19]. Outcomes and controls matter. Full study-level audit not performed; correlation and experimental estimates remain separate.

### P08

[Samur, Tops & Koole (2018; online 2017), Does a single session of reading literary fiction prime enhanced mentalising performance?](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/28095740/). DOI: 10.1080/02699931.2017.1279591. Author abstract inspected: four replication experiments, combined N = 1006, found no immediate mentalising advantage over controls; fiction familiarity remained correlated. Counters a universal single-session benefit, not every possible value of fiction.

### P09

[Miall & Kuiken (1994), Beyond text theory: Understanding literary response](https://sites.ualberta.ca/~dmiall/reading/BEYOND_t.htm). DOI: 10.1080/01638539409544873. Author-hosted sections on foregrounding, empirical relations and individual differences inspected. Theoretical argument reports prior segment-level associations with reading time, strikingness and affect. This is not a new independent replication of their cited Poetics study; stylistic difficulty and failure to comprehend are not interchangeable.

### P10

[Oliver & Bartsch (2010), Appreciation as audience response](https://epub.ub.uni-muenchen.de/20995/). DOI: 10.1111/j.1468-2958.2009.01368.x. University author abstract inspected: three film-viewing studies distinguish fun, suspense and moving/thought-provoking appreciation. Supports separating response dimensions. Direct validation for Korean novels, durable personal change or a specific theme was not established by this inspection.

### P11

[Menninghaus et al. (2017), The Distancing-Embracing model of the enjoyment of negative emotions in art reception](https://research.rug.nl/en/publications/the-distancing-embracing-model-of-the-enjoyment-of-negative-emoti/). DOI: 10.1017/S0140525X17000309. University author abstract inspected. Theoretical model combines an art/fiction frame with mixed emotion, aesthetic form and meaning construction. Not proof that more suffering necessarily produces more appreciation.

### P12

[Doshi & Hauser (2024), Generative AI enhances individual creativity but reduces the collective diversity of novel content](https://discovery.ucl.ac.uk/id/eprint/10195027/). DOI: 10.1126/sciadv.adn5290. UCL author abstract inspected: online short-story experiment found favorable evaluations with AI ideas but greater between-story similarity. Publisher PDF was reached but repeated fetches timed out; detailed methods not audited. Not an evaluation of long-novel quality or LLM judging accuracy.

### P13

[김주·김선남 (2022), 국내 웹소설 이용자의 이용동기에 관한 연구](https://www.kci.go.kr/kciportal/landing/article.kci?arti_id=ART002889938). DOI: 10.21732/skps.2022.108.5. KCI Korean/English abstracts inspected. Survey of 324 users reports multiple motives and associations involving satisfaction and continued-use intention. Observational self-reports concern users and access as well as content; not causal evidence for a chapter formula or actual retention.

### P14

[우정화·주정민 (2017), 웹소설 이용자 속성이 몰입(Flow)과 만족도, 그리고 지속적 이용의도에 미치는 효과](https://www.kci.go.kr/kciportal/ci/sereArticleSearch/ciSereArtiView.kci?sereArticleSearchBean.artiId=ART002285192). DOI: 10.5392/JKCA.2017.17.11.609. KCI abstracts inspected; survey associations among challenge/proficiency, flow, satisfaction and intention. Korean abstract reports 338 users, English 333: unresolved discrepancy retained; no exact analyzed N asserted. No experimental or platform-log evidence inspected.

### P15

[Leavitt & Christenfeld (2011), Story Spoilers Don't Spoil Stories](https://bear.warrington.ufl.edu/brenner/mar7588/Papers/leavitt-Psychological%20Science-2011.pdf). DOI: 10.1177/0956797611417007. Paper pp. 1152-1153, methods, results and conclusions inspected. External spoilers increased enjoyment for tested short stories; inserting them into the story did not show that benefit. Enjoyment, suspense, reader preference and author disclosure choices remain distinct.

### P16

[Speer, Reynolds, Swallow & Zacks (2009), Reading Stories Activates Neural Representations of Visual and Motor Experiences](https://www.psychologicalscience.org/journals/psychological-science/j.1467-9280.2009.02397.x/). DOI: 10.1111/j.1467-9280.2009.02397.x. APS author abstract inspected; neuroimaging relates changing story situations to neural tracking. Supports examining situated actions as a comprehension hypothesis; neural activation does not establish beauty, immersion quality or an optimal amount of description.
