# Evaluate a draft against its intended experience with passage evidence

## Guidance

Use within the existing [manuscript review](../../skills/vegabond-fiction/references/review.md) or [design review](../../skills/vegabond-fiction-design/references/review.md), not as a competing workflow. Accuracy means an auditable fit between a claim, the supplied text, its scope and its evidence. It does not mean an objective meter of literary worth.

### Establish the comparison

Identify the inspected version and span, intended audience, genre/form, desired experience, narrator/character voice and preservation conditions. Use the author and manuscript first; a genre convention is context, not an order to insert conflict or twists. Mark missing chapters and unavailable canon. An outline can support design judgments; it cannot prove sentence rhythm or a completed emotional effect.

Read the supplied span completely. Record what already works and should survive revision. Select the dimensions relevant to the question, rather than scoring every fragment against every possible virtue.

### Build a profile rather than a total score

| Dimension | Passage-level question | Research and craft route |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Causal and information clarity | Can choices and consequences be followed with the information available at this point? | [Situation models](K-FIC-RESEARCH-CAUSALITY.md), plot, viewpoint |
| Character and relationships | What makes this action this person's choice, and what changes between the people involved? | [Perspective](K-FIC-RESEARCH-CHARACTER.md), character, relationships, dialogue |
| Anticipation and payoff | What question stays alive, what matters about its answer, and what does a reveal change? | [Tension](K-FIC-RESEARCH-TENSION.md), buildup, pacing |
| Attention and emotional engagement | What anchors attention or concern; where does the passage invite deliberate distance? | [Engagement](K-FIC-RESEARCH-ENGAGEMENT.md) |
| World and atmosphere | Do rules and lived details shape possible actions and the viewpoint's perception? | Situation models, worldbuilding, atmosphere |
| Expression and voice | Does wording, repetition or a pause earn its place in this exchange? | [Style](K-FIC-RESEARCH-STYLE.md), prose, adapted dialogue |
| Meaning and aftereffects | Which choices, costs or changed images support reflection and alternative interpretations? | [Meaning](K-FIC-RESEARCH-MEANING.md) |
| Distinctiveness and audience fit | Which details depend on this work rather than a generic template; what reader expectation is served or challenged? | P12-P14 below, current work's baseline |

For each selected dimension, report supported strengths, a specific concern, mixed evidence or insufficient evidence. These describe the basis of the judgment, not a standardized scale. Do not average them or assign unsupported percentages. If the user requests numerical ratings, define the work-specific anchors, keep unassessed dimensions unscored and attach textual reasons; do not call the result a validated measurement or predicted audience percentage.

### Make findings reviewable

For a consequential finding, retain:

1. Location and actual observation, with a short quotation or precise passage locator.
2. Intended function and applicable research/craft reference, including its transfer limit.
3. Editorial interpretation, a plausible text-supported alternative and confidence or missing evidence.
4. Classification: demonstrated error, unresolved hypothesis, or improvement/preference; keep strengths too.
5. Smallest useful proposed change, expected gain, preservation condition and likely tradeoff.
6. Recheck of the changed passage and affected dependencies, or the reader question still needed.

Prioritize a small set by consequence for this work: an action the reader cannot follow may need repair before dependent polish. A tasteful but optional metaphor change need not displace the author's voice. Do not manufacture flaws, demand all dimensions be maximized, or keep rewriting after the requested goal is met.

For a consequential plausibility, logic, character-rationality or world-consistency finding, use the [scoped evidence procedure](K-FIC-PLAUSIBILITY-REVIEW.md) and its [research ledger](K-FIC-RESEARCH-PLAUSIBILITY.md). Require the relevant condition and actual disputed passage, distinguish authoritative events from character beliefs, and compare text-supported alternatives. Report a demonstrated error, unresolved support, or no demonstrated error; do not turn missing narration or a nonoptimal choice into an automatic defect. This extends the existing finding classification rather than supplying a new total score.

### Gather reader evidence when the claim needs it

P13-P14 are Korean user surveys: motivation, access conditions and intention differ from actual completion, payment or literary quality. Their results support asking who is reading and under what conditions, not prescribing a universal web-novel chapter length or cliffhanger interval.

When authorized reader feedback is available, record the version, relevant reader familiarity, first read versus reread, reading conditions and whether plot explanations were supplied. Ask neutral, original questions such as: What did you understand each person wanted? Where did you pause or want to stop, and why? What did you expect next? Which choice affected you? What question or image stayed afterward? Separate the reaction from a suggested rewrite. These questions are a custom editorial prompt, not a validated Korean translation of a published scale.

For a useful version comparison, keep the target question stable. Use separate first-read groups when surprise matters, or account for prior knowledge. Blind version labels and vary order when practical. Small convenience samples supply scoped observations, not population estimates; disagreement can expose different intended audiences. Do not claim causality from uncontrolled before/after impressions. This method does not authorize contacting readers or sending drafts to third parties.

### Use AI without surrendering distinctiveness

P12 found both favorable short-story evaluations with AI ideas and increased between-story similarity. It did not test whether an LLM can accurately judge a long novel. Use AI to locate evidence, propose alternatives and check continuity; do not treat repeated AI agreement, an AI's own score or a fluent critique as independent validation. Compare against the actual work's baseline, protect unusual but functional choices, and prefer scene-specific detail over portable aphorisms. No claim of global originality follows from a local reference comparison.

### Korean expression review

When the question concerns Korean wording or voice, retrieve the relevant entry from the [Korean expression guide](K-FIC-KO-EXPRESSION-GUIDE.md). Check scene-grounded mappings, lexical senses and sound, endings and relational stance, recoverable omission and useful repetition. Preserve the manuscript's plain statements and intentional restraint when they work. Do not rate emotional success from metaphor density, a vowel class, a speech level or isolated-word sentiment scores.

## Applicability and exceptions

This is a research-informed editorial method, not a newly validated questionnaire. No manuscript is good merely because every row is filled. Enjoyment can include comedy, wonder, romance, dread, aesthetic attention and quiet recognition; meaning need not be a sermon or moral consensus. Genre, culture, reading history and purpose can change useful tradeoffs. A short extract cannot certify full-novel payoff, long-term impact, worldwide originality, market fit or publication success.

## Checks

- Are the version, scope, goals and missing evidence explicit?
- Does each material finding cite actual text and distinguish observation from predicted response?
- Were alternative readings, successful choices and author preferences considered?
- Does each proposed change target a reasoned mechanism with a preservation check?
- Are experiments, correlations, theory, AI judgments and reader reports kept distinct?
- Are prior drafts preserved when requested, and are dependent scenes rechecked after factual changes?
- Is the final report useful for a concrete next revision without overstating precision?
