### Application

1. State the work, genre, voice, scene purpose and actual question. Retrieve the relevant entry through the knowledge procedure and inspect its sources and exceptions.
2. Choose, adjust or reject the proposed method in the existing brief. Keep the current work's canon and author choices authoritative.
3. Produce the actual outline, scene, paragraph or revision and apply that entry's checks to it. Return feedback-supported corrections through the normal knowledge process.

Themes and resonance can be examined across plot choices, relationships, viewpoint and endings rather than as appended moral speeches. Genre promises and serial chapter boundaries need work-specific treatment; this collection does not establish publishing-market rules or retention thresholds.

### Scholarly expansion and draft evaluation

Added on 2026-11-17: the [research map](K-FIC-RESEARCH-GUIDE.md) routes sixteen inspected papers through situation models, engagement, tension/disclosure, character perspective, style and meaning. It records experiments, meta-analysis, theory and Korean audience surveys separately, with abstract-only limits where applicable. For an evaluation or a consequential revision, consult [passage-grounded evaluation](K-FIC-EVALUATION.md) and retrieve only the relevant topic. Use the work's aims to distinguish enjoyment, clarity, anticipation, aesthetic attention and meaningful appreciation. Record strengths and concerns with passages, alternative readings, the smallest useful change and preservation checks. An outline cannot establish finished prose quality; a total score, repeated AI agreement or research citation cannot establish real reader effects. This addition extends the existing selection-and-review procedure; it does not require every dimension in every scene.

## Applicability and exceptions

This is a bounded foundation, not all of narrative theory, an empirical ranking of writing methods, or a guarantee of reader appeal. Most sources are practitioner teaching in English; adapt questions to Korean diction, genre, culture and the author's style. Wrede's historical examples are prompts, not verified facts about cultures or science.

Evidence classes remain separate: classical poetics; author/hosted teaching and summaries; a university course; direct interview testimony; scholarly concepts; a hypothesis-and-theory paper. Agreement is not independent experimentation. Source access on the checked date does not establish historical source identity. No manuscript or reader panel has validated the proposed procedures here.

Existing work-scoped source references retain their original caveats; this new general corpus does not silently broaden or redate them. In another working project, follow its configured reference scope and current user authorization.

## Checks

- Is the actual question answered, or merely matched to a familiar term?
- Which source claim supports the method, and which part is editorial adaptation?
- Does a relevant exception or different genre change the choice?
- Has the final manuscript artifact been checked separately from the research?
- Can an appropriate reference be found by the listed Korean queries without reading translations?
