# Design relationships as directional beliefs, needs, power and changing choices

## Guidance

### Source-supported basis

Writing Excuses explores dimensions of compatibility and friction, distinguishes personal from positional power, and allows relationships to be planned or discovered during writing [S05]. The directional relationship record below is an editorial extension, not a named model or empirical result from that episode.

### Applied procedure

1. For A toward B and B toward A separately, note desire, trust, fear, debt, mistaken belief and what each knows about the other.
2. Choose specific compatibility and friction rooted in values, resources, habits or humor; not all differences need to become fights.
3. Put the pair under an external demand and identify who can grant, withhold or lose something the other needs.
4. Record the action witnessed and the resulting change in trust, obligation, intimacy or power; keep changes asymmetric when appropriate.

### Exercise and expected artifact

Create a two-direction relationship card before and after one shared problem. Identify the one event that makes each update believable.

### Draw and retrieve the relationship

For a character relationship diagram or an AI-readable record, use the [evidence-and-time map method](K-FIC-CRAFT-RELATION-MAP.md), added on 2026-11-17. It keeps stable IDs, directed types, attributed claims, scene phases and source passages behind selected visual views. The original S05 source check below retains its date and scope.

## Applicability and exceptions

A relationship need not be romantic, symmetric or continually worsening. A stable bond can face external pressure without a manufactured breakup. Material constraints, culture and individual interpretation require story evidence; categories do not determine personalities.

Use the current manuscript, author choices and available context as the authority for application. This entry supplies options for a concrete decision, not requirements to apply every device.

## Checks

- Can both sides' needs and interpretations be stated without assuming shared knowledge?
- What action earns the relationship turn and what remains unresolved?
- Does power come from an established resource, role or personal connection?
