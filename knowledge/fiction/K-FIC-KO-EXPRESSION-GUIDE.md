# Choose Korean expression resources by scene, voice and emotional purpose

## Guidance

Interpret the user's Hangul request as Korean-language expression, not a claim that the script itself causes a particular emotion. Research Korean uses of shared figurative processes alongside its sound-symbolic vocabulary, endings and discourse choices. Do not equate Korean with one national sensibility or claim that metaphor, omission or emotional subtlety exists only in Korean.

| Decision | Reference | Focused lookup |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Turn a scene-specific experience into a simile, metaphor, metonymy or sensory image | [Figuration](K-FIC-KO-FIGURATION.md) | 한국어 비유 은유 환유 공감각 |
| Choose a sound or ideophone without assuming an emotional vowel code | [Sound symbolism](K-FIC-KO-SOUND.md) | 의성어 의태어 음성상징 어감 |

Use [draft evaluation](K-FIC-EVALUATION.md) to assess an actual passage.

### Application

1. Name the emotional tension, speaker/perceiver, listener, scene event, genre and current voice. Select a relevant mechanism instead of searching only for impressive phrases.
2. Choose concrete material available to that person and situation. Preserve what the character can know and what the reader needs to follow.
3. Compare a plain version and a figurative or grammatical alternative. State what changes in emphasis, implication or emotional distance. Several changed variables make this an editorial comparison, not an experiment.
4. Read in context. Keep the option that serves this work, including plain statement or silence. Save useful adaptation reasons through the existing case/knowledge process.

## Applicability and exceptions

This is a purposive Korean-focused collection. K01-K14 are fourteen papers; K15-K17 are three public resources, not additional papers. Most papers were inspected through author abstracts in KCI; K10 also through selected full-paper sections. These access depths support bounded summaries, not full methodological audits or a meta-analysis. Preserve disagreements and differences in measures. A corpus frequency, ERP component, lexical response time, affect rating or translation analysis does not establish that a novel moves readers.

The five topic entries contain editorial applications. No controlled Korean-novel comparison or reader panel validated them. Individual history, dialect, age, genre and situation may change reception. Grammar and metaphor have many uses; do not assign fixed emotions to endings, native vocabulary, Sino-Korean words or phonemes. No actual corpus query or download was performed; K17 provides a future lookup route.

## Checks

- Can the chosen image be understood through a shared feature or meaningful relation grounded in this scene?
- Do endings and honorifics fit the speaker's knowledge, addressee and established relationship?
- Are dictionary sense, corpus usage, linguistic explanation, literary interpretation and actual reader response distinguished?
- Did a revision preserve voice, referents, action and intentional restraint?
- Are source quotations, conventional lexical examples and newly composed examples labeled separately?
- Can a focused Korean query retrieve the relevant entry without loading human translations?

### K01

[전기정 (2023), 한국어와 중국어의 비유 유형 대조](https://www.kci.go.kr/kciportal/landing/article.kci?arti_id=ART002988898). DOI: 10.16874/jslckc.2023..69.003. KCI abstracts inspected. Comparative rhetorical classification discusses similarity, contiguity and categories. Descriptive/theoretical analysis, not a hierarchy of beauty or an experiment showing Korean exclusivity.

### K02

[남윤주·정혜인 (2016), 한국어 술어 은유처리에 대한 ERP 연구](https://www.kci.go.kr/kciportal/ci/sereArticleSearch/ciSereArtiView.kci?sereArticleSearchBean.artiId=ART002130078). Language and Information 20(2), 93-116; UCI G704-000467.2016.20.2.006; no DOI supplied on the inspected record. Author abstract describes offline tasks and ERP comparisons of literal, conventional, novel and anomalous conditions. Processing differences do not measure literary pleasure or movingness; detailed statistical audit not performed.

### K03

[손연정 (2023), 외국인 학습자를 위한 신체 관련 한국어 감정 관용 표현 연구](https://www.kci.go.kr/kciportal/landing/article.kci?arti_id=ART002954385). DOI: 10.22718/kga.2023.7.2.003. KCI abstracts inspected. Cognitive-linguistic classification of selected anger/anxiety idioms through bodily source domains. Pedagogical/semantic analysis, not physiological diagnosis or proof of uniform Korean feelings.

### K04

[홍영지·남예은·이윤형 (2016), 정서가, 각성가 및 구체성 평정을 통한 한국어 정서단어 목록 개발](https://www.kci.go.kr/kciportal/landing/article.kci?arti_id=ART002149809). DOI: 10.19066/cogsci.2016.27.3.002. Abstracts describe 450 rated words and a Stroop application. Rating-study participant accounts differ across Korean/English abstracts; exact overall N unresolved. Raw norms not downloaded or reanalyzed. Isolated-word ratings are not sentence or novel-emotion scores.

### K05

[Lee, Jeongyi (2022), 음성상징 연구: 한국어 음성상징어의 지각에 미치는 음성상징의 공감각적 효과](https://www.kci.go.kr/kciportal/ci/sereArticleSearch/ciSereArtiView.kci?sereArticleSearchBean.artiId=ART002810538). DOI: 10.20405/kl.2022.02.94.87. KCI bilingual abstracts inspected: 54 native/non-native respondents rated ideophones; familiarity and acoustic qualities matter. Favorable evaluations of some sound classes in this task do not imply fixed emotional valence across words or prose.

### K06

[Kwon, Nayoung & Lee, Yoonhyoung (2026), When Words Sound Big or Small](https://www.kci.go.kr/kciportal/landing/article.kci?arti_id=ART003328252). DOI: 10.17791/jcs.2026.27.1.25; Journal of Cognitive Science 27(1), 25-49. KCI author abstract inspected. Explicit vowel-associated ratings and lexical decision results diverged; online findings were mixed. Different tasks and dimensions from K05 must not be flattened into one universal vowel/emotion rule.

### K07

[정경숙 (2016), 지식의 출처관점에서의 '-네'와 '-구나'](https://www.kci.go.kr/kciportal/landing/article.kci?arti_id=ART002135723). DOI: 10.15718/discog.2016.23.3.45. Author abstracts inspected. Semantic analysis contrasts perception and recognition/knowledge sources. It is one analysis, not the final exhaustive account of these endings or a controlled emotional-response study.

### K08

[Chen, Xinling (2025), 담화 유형별 말뭉치 분석을 통한 종결어미 ‘-네’와 ‘-구나’의 의외성·증거성 비교](https://www.kci.go.kr/kciportal/landing/article.kci?arti_id=ART003267173). DOI: 10.20988/lfp.2025.66..409. KCI abstracts inspected: analysis reports 55,075 utterances and context-dependent functions, including strategic evaluation/inference with -네. Reported corpus results were not reproduced here; frequency and stance do not measure affective impact.

### K09

[원은하·김성희·전주영 (2021), 소설 속 대화의 번역: 존대등급 종결어미의 문체적 활용](https://www.kci.go.kr/kciportal/landing/article.kci?arti_id=ART002700665). DOI: 10.15749/jts.2021.22.1.004. Author abstract inspected. Selected English/American novels in Korean translation illustrate speech-style functions beyond politeness. Translation-text analysis does not prescribe one speech level for every age, genre or relationship.

### K10

[권은희·성초림 (2014), 소설 속 대화문에 나타나는 한국어 문장종결어미 번역의 문제](https://journal.kci.go.kr/kats/archive/articlePdf?artiId=ART001887605). DOI: 10.15749/jts.2014.15.2.001. KCI abstract and selected journal-PDF passages from the introduction, §§2.1/2.2.1 and §3.2 inspected. Case comparison of Korean dialogue and two Spanish translations connects endings, speaker identification and concise narration. Not a reader experiment; quoted fiction inside the paper is not reused here.

### K11

[강동희 (2023), 노한 문학 번역에서 나타난 생략과 환원의 양상 고찰](https://www.kci.go.kr/kciportal/landing/article.kci?arti_id=ART002945564). DOI: 10.15749/jts.2023.24.1.009. KCI author abstract inspected. Selected Russian-to-Korean literary translation illustrates retained omissions and context-motivated restoration. Reported percentages are not ideal omission targets for original Korean fiction.

### K12

[박주형 (2022), 은유 확장 중심의 시 읽기 교육 연구](https://www.kci.go.kr/kciportal/ci/sereArticleSearch/ciSereArtiView.kci?sereArticleSearchBean.artiId=ART002876091). DOI: 10.29401/KLE.178.5. Author abstracts inspected. Expert response texts to a specific poem inform metaphor-network and reading-method analysis. Educational/interpretive evidence, not a controlled test that more linked metaphors improve novels.

### K13

[정끝별 (2007), 현대시 리듬 교육에 관한 시학적 연구](https://www.kci.go.kr/kciportal/landing/article.kci?arti_id=ART001070906). DOI: 10.19050/korlit.2007..15.009. KCI abstracts inspected. Parallelism, repetition and semantic differences inform a proposed poetic-rhythm pedagogy. No fixed meter or sentence pattern for prose, and no measured novel-reader effect, follows from this argument.

### K14

[민재원 (2012), 시 읽기에서의 정서 형성 교육 연구 시론(試論)](https://www.kci.go.kr/kciportal/landing/article.kci?arti_id=ART001693054). Literature Education 38, 241-272; no DOI supplied in the inspected record. Author abstract offers a theoretical account of images, prior experience and relational appraisal in emotion formation. Not a reader experiment or a guarantee of a specified response.

### K15

[박동근, AKS 한국민족문화대백과사전, 음성상징](https://encykorea.aks.ac.kr/Article/E0078798). Entry written 2016, page shows revision 2023-07-18. Definition, overview and content inspected. Scholarly reference on ideophones and consonant/vowel alternation; not a universal affect law. The entry states that its author's scholarly view may differ from AKS's official position.

### K16

[국립국어원 온라인가나다, 음성상징어](https://www.korean.go.kr/front/onlineQna/onlineQnaView.do?mn_id=216&pageIndex=1&qna_seq=318291), answer dated 2025-07-16. Answer and cited senses of 울컥 inspected: emotional and physical uses differ. Lexicographic guidance, not a study of literary emotion; the answer's consulted entries do not settle every ideophone's part of speech.
