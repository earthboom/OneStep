# Track support and end state in physical action

## Guidance

For consequential action, establish support contact, force source, motion, braking, and the posture available for the next action. Separate anatomical basics from conditional experiments and fictional propulsion.

## Applicability and exceptions

Apply only within the source's genre, era, equipment, experimental, and verification conditions. Current author instructions, style references, and owning canon prevail. Proposed examples and fictional extensions remain proposals. Recheck the source if its hash or the scene conditions change.

## Checks

When an action needs free hands, trace temporary weapon or prop placement and the later pickup; after greetings, reconsider whether a held pose still serves the next dialogue. Do not label an omitted action physically impossible without excluding supported alternatives.

Check the end of one action against the start of the next, including feet, hands, weapon recovery, injury, and ground condition. No universal optimal angle or reaction time is established.

Before writing, return the useful evidence, applicable conditions, scene decisions, and open gaps. After writing, reread the whole affected scene for causality, continuity, disclosure, and author voice.
