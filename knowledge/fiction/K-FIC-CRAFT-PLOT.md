# Connect narrative structure through causes, choices and reader expectations

## Guidance

### Source-supported basis

Aristotle examines connections between incidents and reversals within tragedy [S01]. The official Sanderson notes distinguish a whole-book problem from local progression and describe expectations established and answered by a story [S02]. These are different historical and aesthetic approaches, not independent proof of one universal structure.

### Applied procedure

1. State what the reader follows: a pursuit, relationship, inquiry, experience, or thematic question; identify the intended genre and ending openness.
2. Map consequential choices and their prerequisites. For each major turn, name what causes it and how the available options change.
3. Use an outline, reverse outline or exploratory draft according to the work. Check beginning, development and ending without imposing act counts or percentages.
4. Trace each subplot to a change in a central choice, relationship, meaning or expectation. Retain deliberate digressions by function rather than deleting them automatically.

### Exercise and expected artifact

Create a one-page sequence of five consequential events from the current manuscript; annotate cause, choice, changed state and the question carried forward. This is an editorial exercise, not a required five-event story.

## Applicability and exceptions

The classical source privileges tragic unity; do not import its rankings of characters, gender or ideal endings. Episodic, contemplative and deliberately discontinuous fiction can organize experience by other means. A promised effect is a hypothesis to check with actual reading.

Use the current manuscript, author choices and available context as the authority for application. This entry supplies options for a concrete decision, not requirements to apply every device.

## Checks

- Can the major turns be reconstructed from the established facts and choices?
- Does the ending answer, transform or deliberately leave open what the opening made important?
- Which apparent structural violation is intentional, and what effect supports retaining it?
